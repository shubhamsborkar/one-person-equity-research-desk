Broker marks, read-only. Margin cushion = total F&O limit (cash + pledged shares) minus blocked margin; ICICI squares off only when the whole limit is gone — there are no per-position stops. The −5% line is a straight beta estimate, not a promise about a gap day. India and US close ~9½ hours apart, so IN↔US correlations understate the true linkage. Illiquid names are carried at their last print; a no-trade day counts as a 0% day, same as the NAV mark.
