Connecting to desk…

Marks: the broker's own live marks, the prices it computes margin and square-off against.

Square-off: the margin-used bars show blocked margin against the account's total limit, the way the shipped adapter's broker reports it; other brokers report it differently, so cross-check the exact limits on your broker's own page.

Orders: this copy reads the account and places no orders. Order routing against your broker's API is your own build.
